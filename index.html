ABOUT TOPCARE

A school parents trust in Busiika

Topcare Primary School - Busiika is a caring Day and Boarding school for Baby Class to Primary 7. We teach children not only books, but also discipline, good manners, farming, computer, and fear of God.

Our teachers stay close to every child. Slow learners get extra help. Bright children get extra challenge. Every child is known by name.
